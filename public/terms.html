1. About the game

LoL Ultimate Roster ("the game") is a browser game made by Studio8Heads in collaboration with SISL, played at lol-ultimate-roster.xyz. It has three game modes: Ultimate Roster (collect cards and build a squad), Ultimate Career (play one pro's career) and Ultimate Manager (run a club).

2. Using the game

By playing the game you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the game. If you are under the age at which you can agree to terms like these where you live, ask a parent or guardian to read them with you before you create an account.

3. Free to play, no real money

  • The game is free. There are no purchases, subscriptions or adverts.
  • Coins, packs, cards, players, trophies, promo-code rewards and everything else you earn in the game are virtual items with no real-world value. They cannot be bought, sold, exchanged or cashed out, and we do not pay compensation if they are lost, changed or reset.
  • We may rebalance, change or remove game content (card ratings, rewards, modes) at any time, including in ways that affect existing saves.

4. Accounts

  • An account is optional. Without one, the game is saved only in your browser.
  • You sign up with an email address and a password. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication (Google); we never see or store your password.
  • Keep your login to yourself. You are responsible for what happens on your account.
  • Use a display name and club name that are not offensive, hateful, sexual, or pretending to be someone else.

6. What other players can see

  • Roster leaderboard: your display name, club name and colours, squad Power, Glory, trophies and Golden Road progress, and the favourite team and player you set in your public profile.
  • Career leaderboard: each career you play with an account is listed automatically with its handle, role, region, clubs, ratings, awards and stats, and anyone can open its profile. You can hide any career from the board on the leaderboard screen; hiding removes its entry and empties its public profile.
  • Friends: players you add as friends can see your leaderboard details.

7. Storage on your device

The game stores your saves and settings (sound, theme, display size and similar) in your browser's local storage, so the game works offline and remembers you. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Firebase may store a login token in your browser to keep you signed in. Clearing your browser data deletes your local saves; anything not synced to an account is then gone for good.

8. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores account and cloud-save data. Google processes it under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts provides the game's fonts, so your browser requests them from Google.
  • Cloudflare sits in front of the website and handles your connection to it, as it does for most websites.
  • Links to other sites (for example our Discord server or our other games) lead to services with their own terms.

9. Deleting your data

  • Local saves: delete them from the main menu's save slots, or clear your browser's site data.
  • Career leaderboard: hide a career at any time from the leaderboard screen.
  • Your account and everything stored with it (email, cloud save, leaderboard entries, friends): ask us on our Discord server (see Contact) and we will delete it.

10. Fair play

Do not cheat. That includes editing saves or game data to fake progress, exploiting bugs on purpose, automating play, or interfering with the leaderboards or other players' data. We may remove leaderboard entries, reset progress or close accounts that break these rules or use offensive names, without notice.
