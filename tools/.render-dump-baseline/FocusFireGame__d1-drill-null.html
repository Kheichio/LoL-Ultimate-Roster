Teamfighting drill

Focus Fire

Target selection, positioning and cooldown discipline when everything happens at once.

Teamfights are decided in the first second, by whoever picks the right thing to hit. This drill freezes a 5v5 the instant before the trade and asks one question over and over: of everything on this screen, what is worth your cooldowns right now? A dying carry beats a healthy tank, a target in stasis is worth nothing at all, and sometimes the correct answer is that you are the one about to die and the button to press is the one that gets you out.

How to play
  • 1 - 5 picks a target, 0, D or Esc calls Disengage. Clicking works the same.
  • Zhonya, untargetable and out of range are zero. The rotation is simply thrown away.
  • Shields, spell shields and heals shrink a target's value hard, but do not zero it.
  • Stunned and rooted targets are worth more - you are guaranteed to connect.
  • Low health x high threat is the core of the priority score. Distance gates all of it.
  • Watch your own bar. If incoming damage beats your health and your escape is up, disengage wins.
  • Second-best scores half. Answer fast - full marks decay as the clock drains.
