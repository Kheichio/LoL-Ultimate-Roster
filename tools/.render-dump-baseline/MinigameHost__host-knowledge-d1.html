Basic Drill

Read the notes properly, then get quizzed on the three changes everyone else skimmed past.

Game knowledge is not a feel, it is a lookup table. Objective timers, item components, gold values and the handful of rules that decide whether your math in a 2v2 is right. Nobody calculates any of it mid-fight - they memorised it years ago. This drill hammers that recall until the answer arrives before you have finished reading the question.

How to play
  • 10 questions, four answers, exactly one correct.
  • Press 1-4 or click an answer. Wrong and out-of-time both count as a miss.
  • 18s per question and 300s for the whole session - questions you never reach still count against you.
  • Consecutive correct answers build a streak, and your best streak adds a bonus.
  • Every answer is explained. Press Space to move on early.
