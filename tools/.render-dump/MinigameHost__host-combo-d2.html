Combo Under Pressure

Full rotation on a moving dummy while the coach calls out a new target every four seconds.

  1. Watch the ring. It shrinks onto the target. Hit it the moment the ring touches the circle — not before, not after.
  2. Click the target, or press Space / Z / X to strike whichever target is closest to its beat.
  3. Keep the combo. A miss resets it, and the best unbroken run is a quarter of your score.
