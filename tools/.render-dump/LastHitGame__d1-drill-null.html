Last Hit Timing

The single most repeated action in the game. A minion only gives you gold if your attack lands while its health is low enough to kill it. Swing too early and you shove the wave; swing too late and your tower eats the gold.

  1. Watch the bar. The marker is the minion's health, draining from right to left.
  2. Press in the green. That band is the kill window. The bright core is a perfect last hit.
  3. Use Space or click Strike. Cannons drain slowly, casters are quick.
