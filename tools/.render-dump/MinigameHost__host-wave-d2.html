Cannon Cadence

Advanced

Three waves, then the cannon, then crash. Fourteen times, without looking at the clock.

How to play
  1. A wave arrives every beat. Your wave walks up the lane and meets theirs at the amber band. Press when it gets there.
  2. HOLD (H) — last hit only. The wave joins your push and the push grows.
  3. CRASH (SPACE) — clear the lot. The whole push slams into their tower and banks as CS.
  4. A bigger push meets them further up the lane, moves faster and gets a tighter band. One wave is a shove and pays under the odds. Three or more is a crash.
  5. Five waves is as far as it goes. Hold a sixth and it crashes on its own and their tower keeps most of it.
  6. Every third wave is a cannon and is worth more. The NEXT strip tells you which ones, before the first beat.
  7. Cash before the lane runs out. Anything still on the lane after the last wave is lost — only what you crashed counts.
