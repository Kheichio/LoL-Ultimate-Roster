    Get to Diamond Scouts start returning calls at 2400 MMR. Below that you are a name in a spreadsheet nobody opened.
  2. 2
    Keep training anyway Unsigned, every attribute crawls past 72 — roughly a seventh of the normal rate, not a wall. A club environment is what lifts that, so train up to it and stop burning weeks on the other side.
  3. 3
    Take the first real offer Even an open-circuit side gives you a coach, a room and a schedule. The cap comes off the day you sign, not the day you sign somewhere good.
